# Creature profiles

The native roster provides the available model definitions. The friendly alliance, speech and personality traits are authored for this mod and are not presented as canonical creature dialogue.

Species notes were checked against these references on September 27, 2026:

- [Wolves](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/wolf), [dogs](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/dog), [bears](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/bear) and [boars](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/boar): valley wildlife and hunting behaviour.
- [Gargoyles](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/gargoyle): constructed creatures and native combat traits. Flying remains unavailable as a player mount action.
- [Tatzelwurms](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/tatzelwurm): burrowing predators and poison.
- [Undead dogs](https://dawnwalkerwiki.com/creatures/undead-dog): guardians associated with former masters and burial places.
- [Boss catalogue](https://thegameswiki.com/dawnwalker/wiki/bosses): Beast of Balaur location and creature type.
- [Enemy catalogue](https://dawnwalkerdb.com/enemies): Mare variants. Specific mythology is not established by that definition alone.

No specific lore was verified for each astral colour variant or each farm animal. Those profiles explicitly leave origins and personal history unknown rather than borrowing mythology from another game. `characters/creatures.json` keeps each profile's sources beside its lore note. The cloud profile also distinguishes these notes from events in the player's current save.

Voices are selected from Convai's live voice list; English profiles use Kokoro and multilingual profiles use Azure. Voice selection is a presentation choice, not a statement about the original creature's sex or canon personality.
